# kmd Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026. This policy covers the kmd app for iPhone and iPad.

## Data collection

The iOS app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, browsing history, or the contents of your documents. It does not track you across apps or websites.

## Documents and drafts

Documents you open remain in the location you choose through Apple's document picker. kmd keeps a protected local recovery draft in its app container so an interrupted edit can be restored. The app does not upload that draft.

## Share extension

When you explicitly use **Share with kmd**, the selected text, link, or file is copied into kmd's private App Group inbox on your device. It is not read until you open kmd and is not sent to our servers.

## Cloud and file providers

If you save a document to iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider processes the file under its own privacy policy. The first iOS release does not connect to the optional account and encrypted-sync services on notes.apuch.cn.

## Retention and deletion

Your documents are controlled through the Files app. Local drafts and the share inbox are removed when you delete the app; items stored in an external file provider remain under that provider's controls.

## Questions

Use [kmd support](SUPPORT.md) for privacy questions. Please do not include private note content in a public issue.
